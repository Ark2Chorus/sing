      This app is intended for offline use. Please follow the steps below to get started.
       
       Android users 
         
      1. Tap  Copied! then open Google Chrome and paste the link in the address bar.
        2.  
      2. In the upper right of the screen, tap the 3 dots to show the menu.
        3.  
      3. Find Install app / Add to Home screen.
        4.  
      4. Close the browser and wait until the download is complete and the app shows on your phone.
        5.  
      5. Open the app using the default PIN “11111” or check your subscription email.
        6.  
       
      By subscribing to this app, you are helping our church building project in “Mandaue City Central Seventh-Day Adventist Church” Maguikay, Mandaue City, Cebu.
       
      Enjoy singing for the Lord.
      To God be the Glory!!!!
       
       
       iPhone users 
         
      1. Tap  Copied! then open Safari and paste the link in the address bar.
        2.  
      2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen.
        3.  
      3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
        4.  
      4. Keep the name “Ark2 Chorus” and tap Add in the upper right.
        5.  
      5. Close Safari and open the app from the new icon on your Home Screen.
        6.  
      6. Open the app using the default PIN “11111” or check your subscription email.
        7.
