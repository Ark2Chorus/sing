# How to use Ark2 Chorus App

## Getting started
- **Install the app** so it works offline: on Android open the link in **Chrome**, tap the **⋮** menu and choose **Install app / Add to Home screen**. On iPhone open it in **Safari**, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Enter your PIN** the first time you open the app. Your PIN decides which song folders you can see. It is remembered on this device, so you only enter it again when it expires or changes.
- **Switch players** by tapping **Ark2 Chorus ⌄** at the top left: **Note Player**, **Audio Player** or **Music Sheet**. Tapping the round logo takes you back to the home screen.

## Note Player (sheet music with voices)
1. Pick a folder from the list (☁ = shared folder, 📥 = saved on this device).
2. Tap a score to open it. The notes scroll as the music plays.
3. Press **Play**. Use the **mixer** at the bottom to change each part's volume, **Solo** or **Mute** a part, or choose its voice.
4. Where available, choose **Rendered** for a part to hear a recorded singer instead of the built-in voice.
5. Use the **tempo** slider to practise slower or faster (70–130%).

## Audio Player (recordings)
1. Pick a folder of songs or accompaniment tracks.
2. Tap a song to play it. Drag the progress bar to jump to any point.
3. Switch between **Thumbnails** and **List** with the buttons on the right.

## Music Sheet (PDF scores)
1. Pick a folder and tap a sheet to open it.
2. Swipe or tap the arrows to turn pages.
3. Use the header to play along: choose a **recording** (🎧) or the score's **notes** (clef), then press its play button. The ring around the button shows progress; press and drag it to jump.

## Saving songs for offline use
- Tap the download button beside a song or score (or **Download all**) to keep a copy on this device. Saved copies show a green check and open without internet.
- Saved copies update by themselves when the shared folder has a newer version.
- To free space, tap the 🗑 button beside a saved item.

## Internet and updates
- The **Wi‑Fi mark** at the top right shows whether you are online. Tap it to test the connection: a **✓** means the internet works, **✕** means no connection.
- When a new version of the app is ready, you will see **Update available**. Tap **Update now** to get it.

## Changing or renewing your PIN
- If your PIN expires, the app shows who to contact for a new one.
- To enter a different PIN, tap the round logo **five times quickly**.

## Need help?
Use the contact details shown when your PIN expires, or ask your choir leader.
